# Database overview

## Purpose

SMART (Stress Metabolite Analysis and Research Tool) is a literature-curated evidence resource designed to organize metabolites reported to differ between stress and non-stress conditions.

The resource integrates information across:

- biological sources;
- host species;
- stress paradigms;
- analytical platforms;
- direction of metabolite change;
- literature provenance.

## Intended use

SMART can be used to:

- identify metabolites repeatedly associated with stress;
- inspect the biological sources in which a stress-associated metabolite has been reported;
- compare evidence across host species or stress models;
- identify candidate metabolites for experimental validation;
- integrate stress-associated metabolite evidence with transcriptomic, microbiome, or phenotype datasets.

## What SMART is not

SMART is not designed to provide a direct quantitative comparison of metabolite abundance across tissues.

A larger number of recorded metabolites in one source may reflect:

- more studies;
- broader analytical coverage;
- different platforms;
- easier sample collection;
- different reporting thresholds.

Therefore, source-level counts should be interpreted as literature evidence coverage rather than tissue responsiveness.

## Interpretation of missing evidence

Absence of a metabolite–source association means that no corresponding stress-associated evidence was identified among the studies curated in the current release. It should not be interpreted as evidence that the metabolite is absent from that tissue or biofluid.
