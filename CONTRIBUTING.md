# Contributing to SMART

We welcome suggestions and corrections that improve the accuracy and completeness of SMART.

Contributions may include:

- correction of curated metabolite information;
- metabolite synonym or identifier corrections;
- suggestions of relevant new studies;
- metadata corrections;
- documentation improvements.

When proposing a correction or new study, please provide the relevant PMID or DOI and supporting evidence whenever possible.

All submissions will be reviewed by the SMART team before incorporation into future database releases.
